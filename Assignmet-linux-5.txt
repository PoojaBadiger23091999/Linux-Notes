Assignment-4 — File Ownership:
-----------------------------
Create a directory:
/project

Create three files inside it:
/project/app.conf
/project/app.log
/project/deploy.sh

Change the owner of app.conf to john.
Change the owner and group of app.log to:
alice:developers

Change the group ownership of deploy.sh to developers.
Verify the ownership and group of all three files.
